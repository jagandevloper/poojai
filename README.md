# ☠️ Dark Occult Pooja 3D — Sacrificial Skulls & Interview Jinx ☠️

A sinister, full-screen real-time **3D dark occult / satanic ritual scenery** built with Three.js. Set on the midnight obsidian ghats under a Blood Eclipse Moon, featuring the **sacrificial photographs of Karthick S & Kamalesh B mounted on realistic human skulls** in the pooja altar, surrounded by burning tallow candles, ritual iron pins, pierced mock resumes, hooded Aghori cultists, glowing pentagram yantra, and infernal hellfire.

## 🎮 Controls & Interactions

| Action | Control |
|---|---|
| **Rotate 360°** | Click and drag with mouse or touch |
| **Zoom** | Mouse scroll wheel or pinch |
| **Pan** | Right-click and drag |
| **Focus Karthick S Skull** | Click Karthick's skull or press `1` |
| **Focus Kamalesh B Skull** | Click Kamalesh's skull or press `2` |
| **Horned Demon Skull Flare** | Click the center demon skull |
| **Hellfire Eruption** | Click the infernal fire or press `A` |
| **Ring Death Bell** | Click the chained iron bell or press `B` |
| **Reset View** | Press `0` |
| **Toggle Dark Drone** | Press `Space` or `M` (auto-unlocks on first click) |
| **Fullscreen** | Press `F` |
| **Replace Photos** | Drag & drop any image file onto the left or right skull |
